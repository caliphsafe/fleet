Live product care at our New Bedford facilities.

Fleet Fisheries' facilities include a 40,000 lb. lobster pool and a live drip system that can hold an additional 60,000 lb. of live lobster and crab. The system filters the water, supports inventory and grading, and is designed to help decrease mortality.
