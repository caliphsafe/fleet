To The Shore

Learn more about Fleet Fisheries facilities located on the New Bedford harbor. We unload our fleet at our own state-of-the-art facility where our initial inspection process begins.

CERTIFICATIONS

Fleet Fisheries is proud of not only meeting all of the industry standards, but also that we constantly exceed them. Many of our customers have asked for and received cooperation for their own food safety audits.

QUALITY ASSURANCE

Our commitment to quality starts at the top. Ensuring our excellence in quality, we use modern technology coupled with decades of experience and knowledge in the industry

TRACEABILITY

Fleet Fisheries, having dozens of vessels in its fleet, is truly a vertically integrated seafood company. We track each of our boats and use this capability to create unique traceability programs for our committed customers.
