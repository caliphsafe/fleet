Best Practices

GENERATIONS OF KNOWLEDGE & EXPERIENCE COUPLED WITH TECHNOLOGY / INNOVATION, SEAFOOD SAFETY, ADHERENCE TO FEDERAL REGULATIONS AND HARD TEAMWORK IS OUR RECIPE FOR QUALITY & EXCELLENCE IN SEAFOOD

Leading the way to enjoy seafood for generations to come...

Recognizing the need for sustainability, Fleet Fisheries works along-side federal agencies, industry associations and the marine life scientific community to manage the fishery more effectively, as well as protecting the ecology of the oceans to pave the way for future generations for years and years to come!

Learn more about these industry agencies please click to visit our Sustainability page . The harvest of the North Atlantic Sea Scallop is also protected for future generations by limiting the size of the scallop being harvested. The increased size of the "rings" on the dredge allows less mature scallops to leave the dredge thus maintaining a balance of the species that will continue to grow. Fleet Fisheries, along with the entire Northeast sea scallop fleet, uses the latest monitoring technology to ensure that are fleet are harvesting within NOAA territories. This technology is essential to the management of the fishery in order to become a recognized sustainable fishery.
