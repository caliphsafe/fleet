Shipping

FROM THE SEA TO THE SHORE TO YOUR DOOR IS WHAT WE DO BEST

Our commitment to quality & excellence continues even when our products leave our 20 Blackmer Street seafood plant.

Our vertical integration continues to our marketing division working closely with our distributors and retailers to develop sales promotion opportunities and product education to the consumer. Due to our harvesting knowledge and management of our trips, we are able to project advertising promotions well in advance.

Fleet Fisheries works with many carriers from DSL, FedEx, American Airlines and many others.

Rest assured that your order is of the utmost importance to us and we strive to always deliver a quality seafood product on time and in the same condition it left our plant -- FRESH & DELICIOUS!
