Fleet Fisheries is proud of not only meeting all of the industry standards, but also because we constantly exceed them!

Many of our customers have asked for and received cooperation for their own food safety audits.

Our facilities are HACCP compliant and also third party inspected. We achieved an overall score of 98.7 (666/675) from one of the world most recognized labs, Silliker Inc. in our "Good Manufacturing Practices and Food Safety Systems Audit."

Fleet Fisheries participates in "Trace Register" , a product traceability program, for customers who require such a requirement.
